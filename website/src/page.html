Familiar tools. A shared foundation.

Built on OpenCloud. Extended by VOLPAROSSA.

OpenCloud provides the familiar file interface. VOLPAROSSA connects it to encrypted fragment storage and recovery through the shared Core.

Explore OpenCloud ↗ Understand the integration ↓

The current integration supports owner-private uploads and recovery in a limited development setup.

    Open your file

Your device retrieves the required fragments, verifies the recovered data and decrypts the file locally.

Open it through the familiar OpenCloud interface.

Reading a file does not remove the stored copies.

Recovery needs the required fragments, your owner keys and recovery records. Decryption happens on your device.

  • Private uploads through the original OpenCloud Files interface.
  • Repeated recovery after a restart, with the source and one storage participant offline.

In development

  • Full account integration, sharing and writable synchronization.
  • Recovery on another device and automatic storage maintenance.
i

Recovery keys and records currently remain on the owner's device. The current integration is not a complete replacement for an always-on OpenCloud server.

Trial evidence and scope

Imported files remain read only. A separate, explicitly enabled owner-private space accepts new uploads. Private files, names and catalogs are not public-cache or training data.
